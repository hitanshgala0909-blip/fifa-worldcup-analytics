WHAT THE BACKEND PRODUCES (data available to the frontend)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

All of these CSVs / JSONs are available and should be consumed by the frontend:

MATCH DATA:
- group_stage_predictions_calibrated.csv
  Columns: group, matchday, date, team_a, team_b, elo_a, elo_b, elo_gap,
           p_a_win, p_draw, p_b_win, predicted_winner, confidence, upset_probability

- official_r32_bracket_predictions.csv
  Columns: round, match_num, bracket_side, team_a, team_b,
           elo_a, elo_b, elo_gap, p_a_win, p_draw, p_b_win,
           predicted_winner, confidence, upset_prob

SIMULATION DATA:
- wc2026_simulation_v3_calibrated.csv
  Columns: sim_rank, team, p_group_stage, p_r32, p_r16, p_qf, p_sf,
           p_runner_up, p_winner, reach_r16_pct, reach_qf_pct,
           reach_sf_pct, reach_runner_up_pct

PLAYER DATA:
- performance_scores_all_1248.csv  (1,248 players)
  Key columns: overall_rank, player, team, group, position, club, age,
               performance_tier, tournament_projection, pos_fantasy_score,
               captain_score, value_score, breakout_score, underrated_score,
               overall_index, form_score, xG_/90, xA_/90,
               expected_wc_goals, expected_wc_assists, expected_wc_contrib,
               fitness, injury_risk, starter_prob, wc_matches,
               clean_sheet_prob, team_r16_prob, team_power_rank

AWARD PREDICTIONS:
- award_golden_boot.csv     → golden_boot_score, xG_/90, expected_wc_goals
- award_golden_ball.csv     → golden_ball_score, tournament_projection
- award_golden_glove.csv    → golden_glove_score, clean_sheet_prob
- award_best_young_player.csv → young_player_score, age

FANTASY OUTPUTS:
- fantasy_captain.csv       → captain_score, injury_risk, starter_prob
- fantasy_vice_captain.csv
- fantasy_budget.csv        → value picks from lower-ranked teams
- fantasy_differential.csv  → low-ownership, high-upside picks
- fantasy_best_xi.csv       → 4-3-3 formation, max 2 per team

INTELLIGENCE REPORTS:
- breakout_predictions.csv  → breakout_score, reasoning (text column)
- underrated_predictions.csv → underrated_score, reasoning (text column)
- explainability_top20.csv  → player, feature_label, shap_value, direction

POWER RANKINGS:
- wc2026_power_rankings.csv → power_rank, team, group, power_score, elo,
                               squad_index, team_xg, avg_form
